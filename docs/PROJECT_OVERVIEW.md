# Project Overview

## Organization
**Basho Valley Education Foundation (BVEF)** is a duly registered charitable Trust with seven Trustees (most of whom are teachers working in Basho Valley), established to improve learning outcomes for children in Basho Valley (located in the Himalayan mountain range at 8,800–11,800 ft altitude, near Skardu across the River Sind).

## Core Program (From `assets/source/About-Us.pdf`)
- **School-Partnership Program:** BVEF signs an MOU with the local Education Department to supplement public school staff with a **Three-Teacher Team** teaching STEM subjects.
- **Efficiency & Sustainability:** Operates in existing school buildings (no bricks-and-mortar spending), employs primarily local female educators for retention, uses offline digital learning resources on laptops, links teacher bonuses to external exam performance, and equips classrooms with a TV screen, two laptops, extra speakers, and solar panels with battery where needed.
- **Current Footprint:** Supporting 1 partner school, planning a 2nd before year-end, with 16 remaining villages awaiting expansion.
